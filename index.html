HOW IBB WORKS

Business requirements become structured campaigns.

A business requirement is converted into a defined campaign. Eligible members can participate, submit their work and receive the reward specified by that campaign when the submission is approved.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Important questions, answered clearly.

What exactly is Indian Business Bureau?

Indian Business Bureau is a business and opportunity network intended to connect business requirements with eligible members through structured campaigns and initiatives.

Is IBB an employer?

Membership in IBB should not be presented as employment unless a separate employment relationship is explicitly established. Campaign participation is subject to the terms of each individual campaign.

What is the ₹101 payment?

₹101 is the stated one-time IBB membership and registration fee. It is not a deposit, investment or payment for a guaranteed task.

Is the ₹101 fee refundable?

Please refer to the IBB Refund Policy for the exact conditions applicable to membership cancellation and refunds.

What happens after I register?

The intended process is: register → complete the ₹101 payment → submit the required transaction reference → verification → membership processing and access.

Are campaigns always available?

No. Campaign availability depends on actual business requirements, campaign capacity, eligibility and other applicable conditions.

Are earnings guaranteed?

No. IBB should not be presented as guaranteeing income. Rewards depend on campaign availability, eligibility, participation, submission quality and approval.

How are rewards determined?

Each campaign should specify its applicable reward terms before participation. Different campaigns may use different reward structures.

Can a submission be rejected?

Yes. Work that does not meet the requirements or approval criteria of a campaign may not qualify for its stated reward.

Can businesses submit requirements?

Businesses can contact IBB through the business enquiry channel to discuss potential campaign requirements and applicable terms.

Where can I find the legal policies?

IBB should provide dedicated Terms and Conditions, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy and applicable Membership/Campaign policies before or during registration.
