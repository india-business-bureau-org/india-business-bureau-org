The Business Model

Simple. Business work becomes structured tasks.

A business has a genuine requirement. IBB structures that requirement into a campaign. Eligible members can participate, submit work and receive the defined reward for approved work.

01
Business Need
A business needs leads, research, outreach, content or data.
02
IBB Campaign
The requirement is converted into clear campaign instructions.
03
Member Task
Eligible members choose available tasks.
04
Review
Submissions are checked against campaign requirements.
05
Reward
Approved work follows the reward terms of that campaign.

FAQ

Everything important, clear.

What exactly is IBB?

Indian Business Bureau is a community connecting people, businesses and opportunities through structured campaigns and business initiatives.

Where can member rewards come from?

Rewards can be defined within individual campaigns for eligible and approved work. Campaign availability and terms vary.

Is ₹101 a task payment?

No. ₹101 is the one-time IBB membership and registration fee.

What happens after I join?

Register → Pay ₹101 → Submit transaction reference → Manual verification → Membership processing and access.

Are earnings guaranteed?

No. Opportunities depend on campaign availability, eligibility, participation, submission quality and approval.
